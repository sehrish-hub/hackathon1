## Why Humanoids? Applications & Challenges

This topic explores the rationale behind the humanoid form factor in robotics, highlighting its advantages for operating in human-centric environments and utilizing human tools. We will discuss various real-world applications of humanoid robots across different industries, alongside the significant technical, ethical, and societal challenges that need to be addressed for their widespread adoption.
