## The Three Pillars Framework

This topic introduces the three foundational pillars supporting the development of modern Physical AI: Simulation (Digital Twin), Perception & Control (ROS 2), and Embodied AI (LLMs + Robotics). We will explain how each pillar contributes to the capabilities of physical AI systems and how their convergence is accelerating progress in the field, enabling robots to perform complex tasks with greater agility and intelligence.
