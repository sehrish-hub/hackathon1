## Introduction to Physical AI

This topic will delve into the foundational understanding of what Physical AI encompasses. We will explore its definition, differentiating it from traditional AI by emphasizing its embodied nature. The discussion will cover the critical aspects that enable AI systems to interact with and understand the real world through physical perception, decision-making, and action.
