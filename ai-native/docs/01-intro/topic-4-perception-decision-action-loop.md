## The Perception-Decision-Action Loop

This topic elaborates on the fundamental concept of the perception-decision-action loop, which is central to embodied intelligence. We will break down how physical AI systems continuously perceive their environment through sensors, make informed decisions based on this input, and execute actions through actuators, forming a closed-loop control system that enables adaptive behavior in the real world.
