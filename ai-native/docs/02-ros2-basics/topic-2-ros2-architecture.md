## ROS 2 Architecture: Nodes, Topics, Services, Actions, and DDS

This topic provides a comprehensive overview of the ROS 2 architecture, explaining its distributed communication paradigm. We will detail the roles of core components such as Nodes, Topics (publish-subscribe mechanism), Services (request-reply), and Actions (for long-running, pre-emptable tasks). A significant part of this discussion will focus on the Data Distribution Service (DDS) as the underlying middleware, emphasizing its role in enabling decentralized, real-time, and reliable data exchange.
