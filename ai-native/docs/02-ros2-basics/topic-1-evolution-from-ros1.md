## Evolution from ROS 1 and Core Concepts

This topic will explore the history and motivation behind the development of ROS 2, highlighting the key differences and improvements over its predecessor, ROS 1. We will delve into the fundamental design principles that make ROS 2 a more robust, scalable, and secure framework for modern robotics applications, including its support for multi-robot systems and real-time performance.
