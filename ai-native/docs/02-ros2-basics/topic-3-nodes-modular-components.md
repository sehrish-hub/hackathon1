## Nodes: The Modular Components

This topic deepens the understanding of ROS 2 nodes as the fundamental modular units of computation. We will explain how nodes encapsulate specific functionalities, promoting a decentralized and reusable approach to robot software development. Examples will illustrate how different nodes collaborate to achieve complex robotic behaviors, such as perception, navigation, and manipulation.
