## Services and Actions: Request-Response and Long-running Tasks

This topic differentiates between ROS 2 Services and Actions, explaining their respective use cases and communication patterns. We will detail how Services provide a synchronous request/reply mechanism for immediate results, while Actions are designed for long-running, pre-emptable tasks that offer feedback during execution. Examples will illustrate when to use each communication type for effective robot control.
